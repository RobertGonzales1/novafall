# Novafall

**[▶ Play free in your browser](https://robertgonzales1.github.io/novafall/play/)** — the full game, no account, no install.

**[Get it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6789433150)** — adds offline progress while the app is closed, iCloud save backup, and notifications.

An idle space-strategy game. Build a fleet, colonize 64 worlds across 16 sectors, and when your run slows down, **ascend**: collapse the galaxy into Dark Matter and start the next cycle stronger.

Three rival empires hold the frontier — the Vex Hegemony, the Krillix Swarm, and the Hollow Court. They besiege your colonies, blockade your trade, and remember every invasion. Choose a government, pick your friends and enemies by creed, buy an uneasy truce, or break them world by world.

- True idle progression — mines, collectors and labs produce while you're away
- 30 ship classes, 64 worlds, a cabinet of 10 ministers, espionage, wonders, a stock exchange and a senate
- NOVA, your ship's AI, always knows the next best move (and has opinions)
- No forced ads, ever. Every purchase is optional

## Web vs iOS

The browser version has all the game content. Two differences:

- **In-app purchases are iOS-only.** Nothing in the web shop can be bought. Dark Matter is earned by ascending and warp hours drop from the daily crate, so those packs only skip the wait — but the six **Precursor Relics** have no in-game source at all, so a browser player cannot obtain them.
- **Saves are per-browser.** Progress lives in that browser's local storage, so it doesn't follow you between devices. The iOS app backs up to your own iCloud.
