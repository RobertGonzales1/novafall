NOVAFALL — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: August 20, 2026 · Developer: Robert Gonzales · Contact: rvg2151@gmail.com

Novafall is an idle space-strategy game. It comes in two forms: the iOS app from the App Store, and the free web edition you can play in a browser. This policy explains what information each one handles, why, and what never leaves your control.

The short version
  • Novafall has no accounts — we never ask for your name, email, or contacts.
  • Your game save belongs to you: on iOS it lives on your device and in your own private iCloud storage; on the web it lives in your browser. We cannot see it or access it in either case.
  • The web edition has no ads, no crash reporting and no purchasing. It does record the same anonymous gameplay analytics as the iOS app, so we can see where new players get stuck.
  • The iOS app collects anonymous gameplay analytics and crash reports to fix bugs and improve the game.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple — we never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The web edition

The browser version at robertgonzales1.github.io/novafall/play/ contains no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchasing. Those parts of the next section apply to the iOS app only.

It does record anonymous gameplay analytics, the same small set of events as the iOS app — reaching a milestone, moving through the opening tutorial, opening the shop. These are tied to a randomly generated identifier held in your browser, never to your identity, and include basic device information (operating system, device type, browser). We use them to find where new players get stuck. Analytics are processed by PostHog — PostHog's privacy policy.

You are asked first, and nothing is recorded until you answer. The first time you open the web edition you get a choice between Allow and No thanks, weighted equally — declining is not the harder button. Until you choose, and if you decline, the analytics software is never started at all: no identifier is created and nothing is sent. Analytics is not required to play, and the game behaves identically either way.

You can change your mind at any time in the game's Settings → Anonymous analytics. Switching it off stops all further events. Clearing your browser data for this site also erases the stored choice and the identifier, and you will simply be asked again.

Correction, August 18, 2026: an earlier version of this page said the web edition sent nothing at all. That was wrong — the analytics described above were active. The page has been corrected rather than quietly amended.

Your progress is saved in that browser's own local storage on your own device. Two consequences worth knowing: it does not follow you to another browser or another device, and clearing your browsing data for this site will erase it. If you want your empire backed up and synced automatically, that is what the iOS app's iCloud save is for.

You can also back it up yourself with a transfer code (Settings → Save Data). The code is your save, encoded as text, and it is produced entirely inside your browser — it is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we could not read it if it were. Where it goes after that is up to you: anyone you give the code to can load that empire, so treat it the way you would treat a password.

GitHub serves the page and, like any web host, processes standard request data (such as your IP address) to deliver it. See GitHub's privacy statement.

What the iOS app collects, and why

Gameplay analytics (PostHog)

To understand how the game is played and where it can improve, we record anonymous gameplay events — for example, reaching a milestone, opening the shop, completing a challenge, moving through the opening tutorial, or watching an optional rewarded ad. These events are tied to a randomly generated identifier, not to your identity, and include basic device information (device model, OS version, app version). PostHog's privacy policy

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the game crashes, a report containing the technical state of the app (stack trace, device model, OS version) is sent to Sentry so we can fix the bug. Crash reports do not include your game save or personal information. Sentry's privacy policy

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store — we never see your payment card or billing details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchase receipts and deliver what you bought (and to restore your purchases on a new device). This involves an anonymous user identifier and transaction metadata (product, price, date), not your identity. RevenueCat's privacy policy

Advertising (Google AdMob) — optional, rewarded ads only

Novafall shows advertising only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad for an in-game bonus. There are no forced or banner ads. Google AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier to serve and measure ads, subject to the permission choices iOS gives you. If you decline, ads are simply less personalized — the game and its rewards work the same. Google's privacy policy

Two separate consents govern this, and either can say no:

  • App Tracking Transparency. iOS asks whether Novafall may access the advertising identifier. Change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. Where the GDPR applies, a consent form from Google's certified consent platform appears before any ad loads, and your choice is recorded there. To change it, email us and we will tell you how for your device — or reinstall the app, which clears the stored choice and asks again.

Your game save (iCloud)

Your progress is saved on your device and backed up to your personal iCloud storage in a private app container. This backup is between your device and your Apple account: we have no access to it, and no copy of it. You can disable it by turning off iCloud Drive for Novafall in iOS Settings. (The web edition has no iCloud backup — see above.)

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent any data.
  • We don't collect names, emails, contacts, photos, location, or health data.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Novafall is not directed at children, and the analytics described above are anonymous for all players.

Data retention

Anonymous analytics and crash reports are retained by the services above per their standard retention policies and are used only in aggregate. Your game save is retained wherever you keep it (your device, your iCloud) and is deleted when you delete it.

Your choices

  • Analytics/crash data: these are anonymous; if you have a concern, contact us and we will help.
  • Ads: rewarded ads are always opt-in per watch; iOS's App Tracking Transparency prompt (when ads are active) controls personalization.
  • iCloud backup: iOS Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud → Novafall.
  • Delete everything: delete your iCloud data via iOS Settings and delete the app; in-game, Settings → Reset erases your save.
