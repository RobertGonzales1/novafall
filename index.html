❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

  • Is my save backed up? In the iOS app, yes — to your own iCloud, automatically. Check Settings → Save Data in-game for sync status. iCloud Drive must be enabled on your device. The browser version saves to that browser only, and clearing your site data erases it.
  • I reinstalled and my empire is gone. Give it a minute on the title screen — iCloud can take a moment to download after a fresh install. Purchases can always be recovered via Shop → Restore Purchases.
  • Do I have to watch ads or pay? No. There are no forced ads, and every purchase is optional. The browser version has no ads and no purchases at all.
  • Why can't I buy anything in the browser? In-app purchases are iOS-only. Dark Matter and warp hours are earned by playing anyway, so those packs only skip the wait. The six Precursor Relics are the exception — permanent boosts sold only in the shop, with no way to earn them in game, so browser players can't get those.
